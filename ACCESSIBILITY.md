# Accessibility statement

This statement applies to https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/.

The launchpad is designed to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. **It has not been independently audited.** Automated checks run
on every change: the page must not scroll sideways at 320px (WCAG 1.4.10 reflow), must have no script errors, and its
filters and menu switch must work. All content is in the HTML and readable without JavaScript. It follows your
system's light or dark setting and respects reduced motion.

Each project has its own accessibility statement; this one covers the launchpad only.

Report a barrier with the [accessibility form](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/cyberdudeuk.github.io/issues/new?template=accessibility_issue.yml).
There is no email route. If you are not happy with the response, contact the
[Equality Advisory and Support Service](https://www.equalityadvisoryservice.com/).

Prepared on 8 November 2026 from the design and the automated checks, not an audit.
